Welcome to the new Live Beats WebRadio blog! This is where I'll share news about the app, updates, and links to interesting things I find along the way.

For a first post I wanted to share something that fits why Live Beats exists. MIDiA Research, a music industry research firm published a piece on September 18 called Gen Z and younger Millennials are turning to radio for music discovery. Its main finding is that 42% of live radio listeners aged 20 to 34 tune in to discover music.

This is what has intrugued me about radio ever since I tuned in to my very first internet radio stream using Winamp and Shoutcast. I was blown away when I heard songs I had forgotten about and discovering old "new" songs. I was hooked since then and I hope I can share that feeling to you with my app Live Beats WebRadio. The app is free with optional purchase to unlock full features. 

Read the original article here: [Gen Z and younger Millennials are turning to radio for music discovery](https://www.midiaresearch.com/blog/gen-z-and-younger-millennials-are-turning-to-radio-for-music-discovery)

If you'd like to try the app you can [get it on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.lbwebradio.radio). Thanks for stopping by and more posts are coming soon.
